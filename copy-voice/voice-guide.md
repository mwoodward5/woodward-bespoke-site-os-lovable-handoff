# Voice guide

- First-person plural for the business ("we've dug in Kentucky clay since 2012"),
  second-person for the reader ("your site's ready when the crew's boots hit dirt").
- Active verbs. Specific nouns. Local geography where possible.
- No hedging: never "professional service" — name the service.
- Never these phrases (full list in `ban-list.json`).
- Testimonials verbatim from `enrichment_sources.gbp_raw` or omitted.
