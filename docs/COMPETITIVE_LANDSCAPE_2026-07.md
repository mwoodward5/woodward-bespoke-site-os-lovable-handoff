## 2. Where the market is weak — the 5 gaps SiteForge exploits

**Gap 1 — Iteration fatigue is the business model.** The vibe-coding cohort monetizes its own failure: Lovable users burn 60–150 credits on AI-created bugs and "bug loops" ([aitoolgrade.com](https://aitoolgrade.com/review/lovable.html)); v0 "charges for failed generations too — when the AI produces garbage, you still pay" ([superdesign.dev](https://superdesign.dev/blog/v0-review)); Bolt error loops consume 7–12M tokens in an afternoon ([taskade.com](https://www.taskade.com/blog/bolt-review)). Even the "easy" builders demand hours of post-generation labor (Wix: "hours replacing placeholder text" — [hostadvice.com](https://hostadvice.com/hosting-company/wix-com-reviews/wix-com-ai-website-builder-review/)). One survey found Lovable satisfaction drops to 20–30% on production projects vs 85%+ on landing pages ([designrevision.com](https://designrevision.com/blog/why-developers-switch-from-lovable-survey-results)). **SiteForge sells the opposite: one shot, graded, done.**

**Gap 2 — Template sameness at scale.** Durable: "all templates look the same regardless of industry" ([wowslider.com](https://wowslider.com/ai-builder/durable.html), [websiteplanet.com](https://www.websiteplanet.com/website-builders/durable/)); 10Web: "every site shares a similar visual DNA" ([rovela.ai](https://rovela.ai/blog/10web-ai-website-builder-review)); v0: shadcn aesthetic convergence ([superdesign.dev](https://superdesign.dev/blog/v0-review)). For a roofer competing against three other roofers on the same block using the same builder, sameness is a conversion tax. **SiteForge's anti-template layout dedup + cinematic hero families make differentiation enforceable, not aspirational.**

**Gap 3 — Fabricated/placeholder content risk.** Every generator ships invented copy and stock imagery the owner must catch: Framer's "generic placeholder copy that the AI does not replace for you" ([superdesign.dev](https://superdesign.dev/blog/framer-ai-review)), 10Web's interchangeable hero copy ([themeisle.com](https://themeisle.com/blog/10web-ai-builder-review/)). Meanwhile the FTC's Final Rule on Consumer Reviews and Testimonials exposes fabricated testimonials to civil penalties up to $51,744 per violation ([kelleykronenberg.com](https://kelleykronenberg.com/when-ai-content-creation-becomes-a-legal-nightmare-the-hidden-risks-every-business-owner-must-know/), [advertisinglaw.fkks.com](https://advertisinglaw.fkks.com/post/102jrz4/using-ai-for-product-reviews-and-testimonials)). No mainstream builder enforces "real proof only." **SiteForge's provenance rule (GBP reviews, real photos, real services only) converts a legal/trust liability into a gate.**

**Gap 4 — Local SEO/GEO/AEO is thin, manual, or beta.** Squarespace injects unchangeable default LocalBusiness schema on every page and omits FAQ schema ([marksmen.studio](https://www.marksmen.studio/blog/squarespace-seo-problems-schema-markup)); Durable reviewers flag SEO depth ([tooltester.com](https://www.tooltester.com/en/reviews/durable/)); Framer/Webflow require hand-rolled schema and llms.txt; Webflow's native AEO product is private beta ([cmswire.com](https://www.cmswire.com/digital-experience/webflow-opens-next-gen-cms-to-all-customers-and-bets-big-on-ai-search-visibility/)). Industry llms.txt adoption is ~10.13% of 300k domains per SE Ranking ([linkbuildinghq.com](https://www.linkbuildinghq.com/blog/should-websites-implement-llms-txt-in-2026/)). AI crawlers can't parse most SMB sites: "your business name might be in a logo image, your address in the footer, services scattered across six pages" ([e2msolutions.com](https://www.e2msolutions.com/blog/technical-seo-gohighlevel/)). **SiteForge ships LocalBusiness/Service/FAQ/Breadcrumb JSON-LD + speakable + llms.txt on every build, by gate — a 100% attach rate against a ~10% market baseline.**

**Gap 5 — Nobody grades their own output.** Across all 13, the only enforced quality metric found anywhere is 10Web's 90+ PageSpeed guarantee — one number ([max-productive.ai](https://max-productive.ai/ai-tools/10web/)). No competitor issues a per-build QC artifact covering schema validity, mobile integrity at 320px, hero composition, motion accessibility, or layout uniqueness. Documented mobile breakage (Framer headlines bleeding off-screen — [superdesign.dev](https://superdesign.dev/blog/framer-ai-review); Wix mobile "cutting things out of view" — [websitebuilderexpert.com](https://www.websitebuilderexpert.com/website-builders/wix-review/)) is the natural result. **SiteForge's deterministic QC grade is a category-of-one artifact: quality you can hand to a customer.**

## 3. Positioning + recommended a-la-carte SaaS pricing

### Positioning statement

> **Woodward SiteForge is the only site generator that compiles a local service business's real footprint — existing site, Google Business Profile, reviews, photos, map, services — into a cinematic, premium website in one shot, then proves it: every build is graded by deterministic QC gates (layered hero, full local schema, AEO/llms.txt, 320px mobile integrity, reduced-motion, anti-template dedup) before it ships.** DIY builders sell you a starting point; vibe-coding tools bill you to fix their own bugs; agencies bill you $3,000+ and three weeks. SiteForge ships a graded, verifiable, one-of-one site the first time.

One-liner: *"One shot. Real proof. Graded before it ships."*

### Pricing logic (anchored to findings above)

Market anchors: DIY subscriptions cluster at **$10–39/mo** (10Web $10, Webflow $14–39, Squarespace $16–99, Wix $17–36, Durable $20–25); vibe-coding runs **$20–100/mo + top-ups** with the worst NPS driver being credit burn on failures; B12's done-for-you setup is **$1,999 one-time + $42–399/mo**; custom local-trade agencies run $3k–10k+ builds. Woodward's own agency ladder ($99 care / $499–1,299 managed) must sit *above* SiteForge SaaS without cannibalization.

Two rules fall out of the research:
1. **Never sell credits.** Credit resentment is the single loudest complaint against Lovable/v0/Bolt. SiteForge's deterministic gates make output variance low enough to flat-price — a structural advantage competitors literally cannot copy without gates.
2. **Charge for the build, subscribe for the care.** The QC report card is the receipt that justifies a one-time price no template builder can command.

## 4. Out-class checklist — 10 verifiable claims (each backed by an enforceable gate)

Every claim below is checkable by a third party against a shipped site, and each maps to a QC gate or engine feature — not adjectives.

1. **"Every hero ships with 6+ composited visual layers with real motion."** — *Gate: hero layer count ≥ 6.* Durable/Wix/10Web ship flat stock-photo heroes; Framer ships wireframe placeholders ([superdesign.dev](https://superdesign.dev/blog/framer-ai-review)).
2. **"Every build validates LocalBusiness + Service + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList JSON-LD before it can ship."** — *Gate: schema validation is a hard pass/fail.* Squarespace's default LocalBusiness schema can't even be edited ([marksmen.studio](https://www.marksmen.studio/blog/squarespace-seo-problems-schema-markup)); no generator enforces validation at build time.
3. **"100% of builds carry speakable/AEO metadata and llms.txt."** — *Gate: AEO artifact check.* Industry llms.txt adoption ~10.13% ([linkbuildinghq.com](https://www.linkbuildinghq.com/blog/should-websites-implement-llms-txt-in-2026/)); Webflow's AEO product is private beta ([cmswire.com](https://www.cmswire.com/digital-experience/webflow-opens-next-gen-cms-to-all-customers-and-bets-big-on-ai-search-visibility/)).
4. **"Zero fabricated testimonials — every proof element traces to a verifiable source (GBP review, existing site, owner-supplied asset), with a provenance log."** — *Gate: proof-provenance check.* Every competitor ships invented placeholder copy; FTC penalties for fake testimonials run to $51,744/violation ([kelleykronenberg.com](https://kelleykronenberg.com/when-ai-content-creation-becomes-a-legal-nightmare-the-hidden-risks-every-business-owner-must-know/)).
5. **"One shot means one shot: the build is graded on first render, and you never pay for our fixes."** — *Engine: deterministic QC + gate-pass guarantee.* Lovable/v0/Bolt all charge users to repair AI-caused failures ([aitoolgrade.com](https://aitoolgrade.com/review/lovable.html), [superdesign.dev](https://superdesign.dev/blog/v0-review), [taskade.com](https://www.taskade.com/blog/bolt-review)).
6. **"Every page verified intact at 320px viewport."** — *Gate: 320px mobile integrity.* Mobile breakage is a documented failure mode at Framer and Wix ([superdesign.dev](https://superdesign.dev/blog/framer-ai-review), [websitebuilderexpert.com](https://www.websitebuilderexpert.com/website-builders/wix-review/)).
7. **"Every animated component honors prefers-reduced-motion."** — *Gate: reduced-motion audit.* No competitor in this set advertises or enforces motion accessibility on generated output.
8. **"No two SiteForge sites share a layout: section fingerprints are deduped against every previously generated site."** — *Gate: anti-template layout dedup.* Direct counter to Durable's "all templates look the same" and 10Web's "same visual DNA" ([websiteplanet.com](https://www.websiteplanet.com/website-builders/durable/), [rovela.ai](https://rovela.ai/blog/10web-ai-website-builder-review)).
9. **"Generation is grounded in the business's real footprint — existing site, GBP, reviews, photos, map, services — ingested before a single section is written."** — *Engine: footprint ingest pipeline.* The 13 majors generate from prompt/questionnaire only; GBP importers (Localo/Dorik/Brizy) produce one-page sites, not premium multi-page builds ([localo.com](https://localo.com/local-seo-tool/small-business-website-builder), [dorik.com](https://dorik.com/google-business-profile-website-builder)).
10. **"Every delivered site includes a machine-readable QC report card — the grade is the receipt."** — *Engine: per-build QC artifact.* The only comparable market claim is 10Web's single-metric 90+ PageSpeed guarantee ([max-productive.ai](https://max-productive.ai/ai-tools/10web/)); nobody grades schema, uniqueness, accessibility, proof provenance, and mobile integrity per build.
