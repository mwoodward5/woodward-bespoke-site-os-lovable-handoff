- `proof/` — smoke test results, deployment aliases, rebuild manifests, screenshots, and kitchen gate output.
- `source-scripts/` — generator, Firecrawl enrichment, smoke, capture, and deploy-related scripts.
- `data/` — lead queue, enriched queue, Firecrawl enrichment output, and shipped/source logs where available.
- `LOVABLE_TEACHER_REVIEW_PROMPT.md` — paste-ready prompt asking Lovable to grade and repair the work.
