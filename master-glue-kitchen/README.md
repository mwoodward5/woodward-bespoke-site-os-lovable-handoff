# Master Glue Kitchen

464 recipes across 13 categories. Companion to `razzle-fx-kitchen` and `site-superpowers-kitchen`.

## Why this exists
The first two kitchens ship ingredients. This one ships **the glue that ties everything together and makes the first prompt look amazing** — even when the client hands you a blurry phone photo and a two-line bio.

## What's inside
- **Content Rescue** — turn bad client assets into editorial
- **Content Extraction** — turn a paragraph into a full site
- **48 Typography packs** — past generic
- **40 Layout systems** — past bento
- **GEO/AEO** — cite-friendly structure for ChatGPT/Perplexity/Google AI Overviews
- **Local/Geo SEO** — LocalBusiness + GeoCoordinates + multi-location
- **Social preview everywhere** — dynamic OG, per-platform aspect
- **40 First-Prompt Polish Rules** — applied unprompted (see `polish-rules.json`)
- **QC Audit** — pre-launch checkers with auto-fix where safe
- **Immersive/Novel** — WebGL, view-transitions, shaders, easter eggs
- **Legal / Deploy / Glue** — the boring-but-required surface

## Non-negotiables
- Never ship placeholder title, broken image, missing OG, missing favicon.
- Always run alt-text sweep + contrast audit + dedupe before ship.
- Always respect reduced-motion, RTL, and 200%% text scaling.
- Always compatible with `razzle-fx-kitchen` and `site-superpowers-kitchen`.
