# REFUSAL CRITERIA

Do NOT apply these effects when:

- **Regulated verticals** (healthcare, legal, finance filings, government forms) — flourish undercuts trust.
- **Motion sensitivity contexts** without a reduced-motion fallback wired in.
- **Text-only accessibility audits** — never rely on color alone to convey meaning.
- **Long-form reading surfaces** (docs, articles) — decor competes with reading.
- **Data-dense dashboards** — motion on charts misleads.
- **Small screens under 360px** for animation-heavy backgrounds — perf/battery cost.
- **AI-generated human faces/mouths** — banned outright in this library.
